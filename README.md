Featured Projects

Healthcare Encounter Utilization and 30-Day Readmission Analysis

Analyzed hospital encounters to examine how prior utilization and clinical complexity relate to 30-day readmission. Built reusable analytical tables and risk categories in BigQuery SQL, then communicated results through an interactive Tableau dashboard.

Skills: BigQuery SQL, CTEs, joins, window functions, data validation, risk segmentation, Tableau

Master’s Thesis: Happiness and Intersectionality

Investigated the relationship between intersecting social identities and happiness using General Social Survey data. The analysis includes data preprocessing, clustering, principal component analysis, logistic regression, and supervised-learning methods.

Skills: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, clustering, PCA, logistic regression, random forest

University Admissions Segmentation

Cleaned university admissions records and analyzed movement through the applicant funnel. Applied dimensionality reduction and clustering to identify applicant segments associated with different enrollment patterns.

Skills: Python, data cleaning, feature engineering, K-means, DBSCAN, PCA, enrollment analytics

Higher-Education Quality Indices Framework

Designed a multidimensional institutional research framework containing 32 weighted components and 262 indicators across student learning, academic programs, faculty performance, student support services, and alumni success.

Skills: Measurement design, composite indices, institutional research, data governance, quality assurance
