Readmission & Risk

30-day readmission rate by Risk Category

Readmission rate: Utilization × Complexity

High Risk encounters (both high utilization and high complexity) are readmitted within 30 days at roughly double the rate of Low Risk encounters — 20.47% vs 9.71%. The utilization flag alone tracks readmission more strongly than the complexity flag alone (20.59% vs 11.95%), suggesting prior emergency/inpatient use is a stronger single predictor here than diagnosis or medication count.

Avg. length of stay by age group

Readmission does not rise smoothly with age. The [20-30) age group has the highest readmission rate in the dataset (14.24%), ahead of every group over 60 — while length of stay does increase steadily with age. This is an observation, not an explanation: it's a pattern worth investigating further (e.g. differences in follow-up care access by age), not evidence of a cause.
